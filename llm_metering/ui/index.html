What we are investigating

Several hundred agents share one provider quota through the same SDK. Load is flat most of the time and spikes during work hours. Our dashboard tracks requests per minute and shows plenty of headroom even at peak — yet the slowest calls take around forty seconds. Something is throttling us that the dashboard cannot see.

How to use this: pick one of five candidate explanations, set the parameter that governs it, and run scheduler policies against it. A run matches when it reproduces both observed numbers at once — a typical call near 1s and a worst-1% near 40s. That pairing is the bar a candidate has to clear to stay believable; hitting either number alone is easy and means nothing.
